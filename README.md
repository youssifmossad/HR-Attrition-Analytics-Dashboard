# 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges organizations face due to its impact on productivity, recruitment costs, and workforce stability.

This project transforms raw HR data into an executive-level analytics solution using **Power BI**, enabling HR managers and executives to identify attrition trends, monitor workforce KPIs, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

✔ Analyze employee attrition trends

✔ Identify key drivers of employee turnover

✔ Monitor HR KPIs

✔ Detect high-risk employee groups

✔ Support strategic workforce planning

✔ Enable data-driven HR decision-making

# 📊 Key Insights

- Employees working overtime showed significantly higher attrition rates.
- Employees with lower job satisfaction were more likely to leave.
- Sales had the highest employee attrition among all departments.
- Frequent business travel contributed to increased employee turnover.
- Employees in lower salary bands exhibited higher attrition risk.
- New employees were more likely to leave within their early years.
